\chapter{Implementation-dependent Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The following limitations are in effect for \textsc{x86-64} platform implementation:

\begin{itemize}
\item the range of representable integers is \newline [-4611686018427387904..4611686018427387903] (63-bit signed in two-complement representation);
\item the maximal length of array/string/number of S-expression parameters is 2305843009213693951 (61-bit unsigned integer); in practice
  it is further limited by the size of the heap;
\item only the first 10 characters of an S-expression constructor name are taken into account: constructors whose names share
  them are indistinguishable (the source-level interpreter, option \lstinline|-i|, compares full names).
\end{itemize}
